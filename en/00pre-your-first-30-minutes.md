# Your first 30 minutes with AI: where to start

**Time:** 30 min (including sign-up)

---

## Who this lesson is for

This lesson is for you if you:
- Have never opened ChatGPT or Claude
- Aren't sure what "chatting with AI" even means
- Worry you'll "break something on the computer"
- Don't know where to get AI or what it costs
- Keep hearing about AI but have never tried it
- Are a grandparent, a manager outside tech, a high school student, or anyone else who's just starting out

If you've already spent at least an hour talking to AI, skip ahead to the next lesson, [AI without fear](00d-ai-without-fear.md).

---

## The gist

Opening AI for the first time is like a 90-year-old making her very first video call. It feels scary. It seems complicated. It seems like you need to know "something special." In reality, it's three buttons.

In 30 minutes you will:
1. Sign up at claude.ai (you'll need an email address)
2. Ask your first 3 questions
3. See 7 everyday tasks AI handles in a minute
4. Learn what not to do
5. Decide where to go next

🎨 **Picture this:** you're learning to ride a bike. No theory about gears, aerodynamics or the physics of rotation. You get on, push off and ride. Falling isn't the end of the world; a bruise heals. AI works the same way.

---

## What AI actually is (in one sentence)

AI is a conversation partner that has read almost everything written on the internet and can now answer your question. Like a very well-read consultant, except it's free and available around the clock.

It can:
- Explain complicated things in plain words
- Write texts (emails, posts, articles)
- Translate from almost any language into another
- Do math, make plans, come up with ideas
- Make sense of your documents and photos

It can NOT (in a regular chat, without connected apps and permissions):
- Make phone calls for you
- Buy groceries
- Remember you tomorrow if you're not logged in
- Guarantee it's right (it sometimes makes things up, so check important facts)

## Step 1: Go to claude.ai (5 minutes)

**Link:** [https://claude.ai](https://claude.ai)

What to do:
1. Open any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, any of them)
2. In the address bar at the top, type **claude.ai** and press Enter
3. You'll see a sign-up button, **"Sign Up"** or **"Continue with email"** (the wording may differ a little; either way it means "create an account"). Click it
4. Enter your email (any address works: Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo)
5. If you're asked for a password, make one up (at least 8 characters) and write it down somewhere safe
6. Check your inbox: you'll get an email with a 6-digit code
7. Enter that code back on claude.ai
8. Done. You're in.

**Cost: $0 (zero dollars).** The free plan works without a credit card, without a subscription, without a catch.

If Claude ever isn't available to you, for example while you're traveling abroad, the steps in this lesson work the same way in another assistant: see the comparison in [Comparing AI models](00c-ai-models-comparison.md), and current plans and supported countries on the [What's current](https://aimayak.com/now/) page.

🎨 **Picture this:** it's like signing up for any new app: email, a code from your inbox, and you're in. That's it.

### What you'll see on the screen

Claude's main screen is simple:
- **At the bottom**: a box where you type your question
- **On the left**: a list of your past conversations (empty for now)
- **Top right**: your profile icon and settings
- **In the middle**: a greeting and a couple of example questions

No complicated menus. It's a chat, like texting a friend, except there's only one person on the other end (the AI).

## Step 2: Your first 3 questions (10 minutes)

**The main rule:** write the way you'd text a friend or a nephew. Don't try to "phrase it perfectly." AI understands everyday language.

### Question 1: "Hi. What can you do?"

Just type that into the box and press Enter (or click the arrow on the right).

Claude will answer in the language you wrote in. Read the whole answer. It gives you a sense of what it can do, in its own words.

### Question 2: "Explain in plain English what a blockchain is"

(or pick any topic you've always wanted to understand: quantum physics, mortgages, Bitcoin, how AI works, the stock market)

Claude will explain it without jargon. If it's still unclear, type: **"Explain it even more simply, like I'm in fifth grade."** It will rephrase.

### Question 3: "Translate into Spanish: Hello, how do I get to the farmers market?"

(or into French, Chinese, any language)

Claude translates instantly. You can follow up with: **"How do I pronounce that?"** and it will spell out the pronunciation.

**Congratulations.** You just used AI. That's all there is to it. Everything after this is just more kinds of tasks.

## Step 3: 7 everyday tasks for your first few days

Here's what people typically do in their first weeks. Try one task a day, and in a week you'll be a confident user.

| # | Task | What to type | What it replaces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Translation** | "Translate into Spanish: [text]" | Google Translate |
| 2 | **Explaining something hard** | "Explain in plain English what [term] means" | Googling and reading 5 articles in a row |
| 3 | **A list or a plan** | "Give me 10 gift ideas for my mom, who's turning 65" | An hour of thinking on your own |
| 4 | **Proofreading** | "Check this text for mistakes: [paste text]" | That one relative who's good with grammar |
| 5 | **Emails** | "Write a polite email to my manager: I'd like to take time off from June 15 to June 25" | Agonizing over the wording |
| 6 | **Summaries** | "Summarize this in 5 sentences: [paste a long text]" | 30 minutes of reading |
| 7 | **Brainstorming** | "Help me come up with a name for a bakery in a small town" | Sitting with a blank sheet of paper |

**Tip:** don't try to do everything in one day. One task a day is a good pace. In a week, that's 7 wins.

## Step 4: What NOT to do in your first 30 minutes (or ever)

The list is short, but it matters.

❌ **Never give AI your passwords, credit card numbers, Social Security number, bank account details or PINs.** Never. Even if it asks. AI has no reason to know them. If someone "on behalf of AI" asks for your card details, it's a scam.

❌ **Don't treat medical advice as 100% reliable.** AI can explain what aspirin is, but **a doctor makes the diagnosis**. Use AI to prepare questions for your doctor, not to replace your doctor.

❌ **Don't treat legal advice as 100% reliable.** AI can give you the general idea of a law, but **a lawyer goes to court for you**. For any document you sign, talk to a lawyer.

❌ **Don't panic if AI gets something wrong.** It isn't perfect. Sometimes it makes up facts (this is called a "hallucination"). Double-check important numbers and dates with a Google search or with a real person.

❌ **Don't worry about "breaking" it.** There's no red button that deletes the internet. You can click any button in Claude's interface and nothing will break. The worst that happens is you close a chat and open a new one.

❌ **Don't give AI documents with other people's personal information** (a friend's ID, a relative's medical records). That violates their privacy.

## Step 5: Plans: Free, Pro, Max, API

Once you like it, the question comes up: should you pay? Here's an honest comparison.

### Free: $0/month

- Limits: there's a usage limit on messages; the exact number depends on demand and on how long the conversation is. For most people, it's enough
- Model: a standard one (still smart, just not the smartest); which one exactly, see [What's current](https://aimayak.com/now/)
- Good for: 90% of people who are just starting
- **Not a fit:** if you want to work with AI 4 to 8 hours a day

🎨 **Picture this:** the bus. It goes to the same places a cab does, just with stops.

### Pro: $20/month (as of October 2026; less if you pay yearly)

- Limits: noticeably higher than on Free
- Model: access to stronger models in the Claude family than on the free plan
- Bonus: easier to work with large documents (PDF, Word, Excel) thanks to the higher limits
- Bonus: access to Claude Code (that's for people who build software)
- Good for: students, professionals, active users

🎨 **Picture this:** a cab. You ride whenever you want. More convenient. Costs more.

### Max: from $100/month (as of October 2026)

- Limits: several times higher than on Pro (there are 5x and 20x tiers)
- Model: the same models as Pro, with a lot more room to use them
- Good for: programmers, researchers, copywriters, people who work with AI 8 hours a day
- **Not a fit:** regular users (it's overkill)

🎨 **Picture this:** a private driver. Always on hand. Expensive. But if you're on the road all day, it pays for itself.

### API: pay as you go

- Price: charged per million tokens and depends on the model; in English, 1 token is about 0.75 of a word. Current prices: [What's current](https://aimayak.com/now/)
- This is for **developers** who build AI into their own apps
- As a regular user, **you don't need it**. Ignore it until you start building software.

🎨 **Picture this:** renting a car by the hour. You only pay for what you actually use. But you need a "license," meaning you need to know how to program.

**Current plans:** [https://claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing) and [What's current](https://aimayak.com/now/)

**Tip for beginners:** start with Free for 2 weeks. If you keep hitting the limits, move to Pro. You don't need Max unless you work with AI for many hours every day.

## Common questions from first-time users

**"If I close the browser, will I lose the conversation?"**
No. All your conversations are saved in your account, in the list on the left. Come back tomorrow and they'll be there.

**"Does Claude remember me?"**
Within one conversation, yes, it remembers the context. Between separate conversations, no, it starts from a clean slate. If you want it to remember things across chats, turn on Memory in the settings and check that it's available on your plan.

**"Do I have to write in perfect English?"**
No. Typos and casual phrasing are fine; Claude will understand you. It also works in many other languages, so you can write in Spanish or another language you're comfortable with.

**"What if it gives me the wrong answer?"**
Just write: "That's not it. I meant [your clarification]." It will rephrase. You can correct it as many times as you like.

**"Can I break something by clicking the wrong thing?"**
No. There are no dangerous buttons in Claude's interface. The worst you can do is delete an old conversation, and even then you'll get a "Delete this chat?" confirmation first.

**"Do people at Anthropic read my conversations?"**
It depends on your settings. On Free, Pro and Max, Anthropic uses your chats to train its models only if the setting to help improve Claude is turned on. You can turn it off in the [privacy settings](https://claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls); with it on, your data is kept for up to 5 years, with it off, for 30 days (as of October 2026). If you're worried, don't write anything there you'd want to keep private.

## Key takeaways

> AI isn't a complicated technology for programmers. It's a conversation partner in your browser at claude.ai. Free. No credit card. Five minutes to sign up, and you're a user.

> The first 30 minutes are simple: sign up → 3 questions → understand the limits → choose where to go next. No magic. No terms you need to memorize. And if you run into a word you don't know, look it up in the [Glossary](00-glossary.md).

> The main rule: write to AI the way you'd write to a friend. No perfect wording, no fear of getting it wrong. AI is more patient than any person: it rephrases, it re-explains, and it never gets tired.
