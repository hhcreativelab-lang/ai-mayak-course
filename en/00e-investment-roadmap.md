# How much to spend on AI: $0 to $2,000 a month

**Time:** about 25 min reading + 15 min budget planning

> ⚠️ **Prices in this lesson are as of October 2026, and they change often.** For current prices and plans, see the [What's current](https://aimayak.com/en/now/) page. Where we haven't verified an exact price, the lesson says "check the website." This lesson isn't financial advice and doesn't promise any income: the levels below are levels of spending, not a forecast of revenue.

---

## The gist

What you spend on AI **doesn't scale in a straight line**. That's the main thing to understand before you open your wallet.

- $0/month: free plans. Enough to figure things out and handle everyday tasks
- $20/month: adds higher limits and Claude Code
- $50/month: content tools (voiceover, images, translation)
- $200/month: real working capacity for daily work and your own products
- $500/month: several client projects at the same time
- $2,000+/month: a team and scale (enterprise)

The higher you go, the less each extra dollar adds. So the rule is simple: **don't buy more than you need right now**. AI tools aren't a "buy everything and figure it out later" kind of purchase. The approach is "figure out where you are → buy the tier you need → upgrade when you hit the ceiling."

If you use AI in your own work and aren't building your own products, you only need Tiers 0-2. Tiers 3-5 are written for people who build their own services and handle clients, so feel free to skim them.

🎨 **Picture this:** AI tools are like clothes. A basic wardrobe ($50): jeans, T-shirts, sneakers. A business suit ($200): for the office. A tuxedo and a fancy coat ($2,000): for rare occasions. If you go to an office every day, you need the suit. If you work from home writing code, jeans will do. **Buying what you don't need = money hanging in your closet with the tags still on, because you never wear it.**

## 🎯 Decision tree: "how much should I pay?" (the quick answer)

```
Just trying it out, testing for the first time
  → Tier 0 ($0/month: free plans)

Using AI every day and hitting the free plan's limits
  → Tier 1 ($20/month: Claude Pro or similar)

Making content or doing marketing for clients
  → Tier 2 (up to $50/month: a subscription plus content tools)

Building a product or working with AI 6-8 hours a day
  → Tier 3 ($200/month: Max level)

Running an agency with several clients
  → Tier 4 ($500/month: builder)

A team / many clients / software sold to businesses (B2B SaaS)
  → Tier 5 ($2,000+/month: Enterprise)
```

Below: what each tier includes, who it's for and **what you DON'T need to buy**.

## Tier 0: $0/month (free plans)

### Who it's for

- A high school or college student trying AI for the first time
- A retiree who wants to see what all the fuss is about
- A professional testing things out before paying
- Anyone on a very tight budget (where $20 a month is real money)

### What you get (as of October 2026)

| Tool | What's free | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Claude.ai Free** | Chat with a message limit, a standard model, web search, artifacts | https://claude.com/pricing |
| **ChatGPT Free** | Chat with limits; by default a lightweight model from the GPT-5.6 family (Luna) | https://chatgpt.com |
| **Gemini Free** | Chat on Gemini 3.6 Flash, with limited access to Gemini 3.1 Pro | https://gemini.google.com |
| **DeepSeek** | Free chat on the current V4 lineup (data is stored in China, so don't paste anything sensitive) | https://chat.deepseek.com |
| **Ollama (runs locally)** | No limits: open models running on your own computer | https://ollama.com |
| **HuggingChat** | Free chat with open models | https://huggingface.co/chat |

The companies don't publish fixed message limits, and they change them, which is why the table doesn't list them.

### What $0 covers

- Everyday questions like "explain X" or "translate Y"
- Writing short texts
- Simple coding tasks (if you can live with the limits)
- Homework and study tasks for high school and college students

### What you CAN'T do on this tier

- ❌ **Real production work** (a live product that real people use): you'll run through the limits fast
- ❌ **Claude Code**: it isn't available on Claude's free plan (this is the biggest downside)
- ❌ **API access for your own scripts** (the API is how your own programs talk to AI directly): it needs a separate billing account
- ❌ **Voiceover for commercial projects**: the free ElevenLabs plan doesn't include a commercial license
- ❌ **Long documents**: the amount of text you can work with is limited

### When to move to a paid tier

When you use AI regularly and hit the free plan's limits several days in a row, to the point where it gets in the way of your work. That's your signal.

## Hidden costs (the ones people forget)

### 1. Setup time (once, at the start)

| Tier | Setup hours | Equivalent at $30/hour |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 0-1 | 5-10 hours | $150-300 |
| Tier 2 | 10-20 hours | $300-600 |
| Tier 3 | 20-40 hours | $600-1,200 |
| Tier 4 | 40-80 hours | $1,200-2,400 |
| Tier 5 | 80-200 hours | $2,400-6,000 |

These are the author's rough estimates; everyone's experience is different.

**This isn't a cash expense**, but it is an **opportunity cost**: time you could have spent on paid work.

### 2. Learning (the first 6 months)

- Courses (free ones on YouTube + paid ones at $50-500): 20-50 hours
- Documentation for Claude / OpenAI / Cloudflare: 30-80 hours
- Experiments and failed attempts: 50-150 hours

**Equivalent:** 100-280 hours, or $3,000-8,400 over the first 6 months if you count your own time at the same $30 an hour. This is also the author's estimate.

### 3. A buffer for experiments and mistakes

**Rule:** plan **20% extra on top of your budget** for:
- Unplanned tier upgrades (suddenly you need Claude Max)
- Failed experiments (you bought Midjourney and it didn't work out)
- API overages (you went over budget because of a bug in your code)

**Example:** you planned $200/month → in the first 3 months you'll actually spend about $240/month.

### 4. Taxes (if you're earning)

Taxes depend on where you live, how you're set up (self-employed and paid on 1099s, a sole proprietor, an LLC) and your overall situation, so this lesson doesn't give any percentages. If you start bringing in income, talk to an accountant or tax professional early to find out what applies to you, and build taxes into your prices.

**Don't forget:** the listed price of an AI subscription may not include tax. Depending on where you are, sales tax or VAT can be added on top (VAT especially applies to customers in the EU).

### 5. Refund risk and a cash-flow cushion

- Refunds and disputed payments (chargebacks) happen, especially with new clients
- Payment processors can hold your payouts while a dispute is open
- When you refund a payment, the processing fee usually isn't returned to you

**Rule:** keep **1-2 months of expenses** in reserve when you're starting out.

## What NEVER to buy (at any level)

- ❌ **Expensive courses with no "last updated" date**: AI changes fast, and material goes stale within months. Look at the update date and the syllabus; free materials and official documentation cover a lot.
- ❌ **Cloud GPU subscriptions for running models** (unless you're fine-tuning): the API is usually cheaper and faster at small volumes
- ❌ **"AI tools bundle" subscriptions** with 20 tools for one payment: you'll use 2-3, and the rest are there just to pad the list
- ❌ **Paid plugins and extensions you haven't vetted**: many go out of date or shut down (for example, the old ChatGPT plugins have already been replaced by other mechanisms)
- ❌ **Lifetime deals on AppSumo** for your core tools: a company can shut down, and the deal disappears with it
- ❌ **Courses that promise fast income** ("an AI agency in a box" and the like): there's no such thing as guaranteed income, and the money is better spent on real tools and your first projects
- ❌ **Premium AI templates** ($50-500 for a prompt pack): your assistant can help you write your own, fitted to your task
- ❌ **Several AI chat subscriptions at once** on Tiers 0-2 (Claude Pro + ChatGPT Plus + Google AI Pro = about $60/month for largely overlapping features)

## Migration path: moving between tiers

### Signals to upgrade (time to pay more)

- 🔴 **You hit Claude Pro's limits 3+ days in a row** → time for Claude Max
- 🔴 **A new paying client** joins your existing ones → time for Tier 4
- 🔴 **Your own production Worker** is serving real users → time for the Anthropic API
- 🔴 **Your team hires a second person** → time for team licenses

### Signals to downgrade (time to pay less)

- 🟢 **You haven't opened a tool in 30+ days** → cancel the subscription
- 🟢 **A free plan covers your current volume** → downgrade the paid one
- 🟢 **API usage is $5/month out of a $50 budget** → cut the API budget
- 🟢 **You closed a project** that used hosting → cancel the hosting

### The 30-day rule

After you upgrade a tier, **watch for 30 days** before upgrading again. Often after the first month it becomes clear: either you're using everything you pay for, or you haven't really hit the ceiling yet (in which case, downgrade back).

## Key takeaways

> **What you spend on AI doesn't scale in a straight line.** Each next level of spending adds less and less capability. Don't buy more than you need right now; otherwise it's just money hanging in the closet.

> **Tier 1 ($20 for Claude Pro, or $17 billed yearly) is a sensible start for anyone who needs Claude Code.** Claude Code doesn't work on the free plan. Everything else gets added as you need it. If you'd rather use a different assistant, pick one from the Tier 1 table.

> **The model landscape changes every few months.** The current list of Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini models and API prices lives on the [What's current](https://aimayak.com/en/now/) page, not in the text of this lesson.

> **Hidden costs matter more than the visible ones.** Setup time, learning, a 20% buffer for experiments, taxes: together they can add up to several times more than the subscriptions themselves. Plan with that in mind.

> **The upgrade signals are clear:** 3+ days of hitting the limits → move up a tier. The downgrade signals are just as clear: 30+ days without opening a tool → cancel. A regular audit every 3 months helps you catch quiet leaks.

> **Important changes in 2026:** older Claude models (Opus 4, Opus 4.1, Sonnet 4, Haiku 3.5) have been retired from the Claude API; Haiku 4.5 may be retired no earlier than October 15, 2026; GPT-5.5 leaves ChatGPT on October 14, 2026; Copilot Pro has been replaced by Microsoft 365 Premium; since June 1, 2026, GitHub Copilot counts chat and agent use in token-based credits; OpenAI has shut down Sora, DALL-E 2 and 3 in the API, and the Assistants API.
